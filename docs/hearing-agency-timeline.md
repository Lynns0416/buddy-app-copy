# Hearing: Agency PDF / Tour Timeline

Questions for the team before building the tour timeline feature.

1. What data goes in the PDF? (roster, schedule, itinerary, contact info, other?)
2. Who creates it — us or the agency?
3. When does the agency need it, and how far before the tour?
4. Does the agency edit it, or just pass it on to the school?
5. Who is the final recipient — the school? Someone else?
6. Is the format fixed (their template) or can we define it?
7. Would a PDF export from our app satisfy them, or do they need a specific format?
8. Any chance the agency would use our app directly instead of receiving a PDF?
